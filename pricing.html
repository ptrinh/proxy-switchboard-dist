Questions

What exactly am I buying?

A license key that unlocks Pro features in the Proxy Switchboard desktop app — a finished, premade software product. There is no consulting or custom-development component.

How do I activate it?

After checkout you receive a license key by email. Enter it in the app under Settings → About → Enter License. You can also remove the license from a machine there to move it to another.

Subscription or one-time?

Lifetime is a single one-time payment. Annual is billed yearly and mainly governs access to updates — if an Annual license lapses, the app keeps working and only update access pauses.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — see our Refund Policy. Because of the free 45-day trial, we ask that you evaluate the app fully before purchasing.
