Privacy Policy

Proxy Switchboard · Effective date: 17 October 2026

Proxy Switchboard (“the app”) is a per-destination network router for Android, iOS, and macOS. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The app is built to keep your traffic and your configuration on your device; the few things that do leave it are listed below.

Summary

We do not sell your data, and there is no advertising and no cross-app tracking. Your traffic, your proxy settings and your secrets stay on your device. Three things can send limited technical data off it: optional crash reporting, Google's barcode scanner on Android, and in-app purchases. Each is described below.

What the app stores, and where

  • Proxy configurations and routing rules (proxy addresses, hostnames, CIDRs, routing modes) are stored only on your device.
  • Secrets — proxy passwords, SSH private keys and passphrases, and WireGuard keys — are stored in the device's secure storage: the Keychain on iOS/macOS, and encrypted storage backed by the Android Keystore on Android. They are shared only between the app and its bundled network extension/VPN service so the proxy engine can use them, and are never sent to us.
  • Configuration is shared between the app and its network extension through a private container on your device.

Network traffic

When you turn the proxy on, the app routes your network traffic according to the rules you configure, sending each connection either directly or through a proxy server that you specify and control. This processing happens entirely on your device. We do not see, inspect, log, store, or transmit your traffic or your browsing history. Any proxy servers you configure are operated by you or by third parties you choose; their handling of traffic is governed by their own policies, not this one.

DNS

To route by hostname, the app answers DNS queries locally on your device and maps names to internal placeholder addresses. These lookups are processed on-device and are not recorded off-device.

Camera (Android)

On Android the app can use the camera, only when you open the QR scanner, to import proxy configurations from QR codes. Frames are processed on-device and are never stored, recorded, or uploaded. The app works fully without camera access.

The scanner itself is Google's ML Kit, delivered through Google Play services. While it runs, ML Kit reports technical data to Google: this app's package name and version, your device model and OS, a per-installation identifier, and performance metrics such as latency. Google states that it sends this over HTTPS and does not pass it to third parties. This applies to Android only; the iOS and macOS builds do not include ML Kit.

Crash reporting (optional, off by default)

The app can send crash reports, but only if you switch that on. Nothing is sent while it is off, which is how it ships. When it is on, reports go to a GlitchTip instance that we operate at glitchtip.trinh.uk. A report contains the stack trace, the app version, your OS version and device model. We disable the library's personally-identifying context, and strip request and server details before sending. Turning the setting off stops it.

Per-app routing rules (Android)

When you create a rule that applies to specific apps, the app reads the list of launchable apps on your device to show you a picker. That list stays on your device, and the names you pick are saved in your configuration. If you turn on the LAN configuration editor, that configuration — including any app names used in rules — is served to browsers on your local network, so that you can edit it from another machine.

Your public IP address

To show your current public address, the app asks api.ipify.org. That request necessarily reveals your IP address to ipify, who operate that service, not to us.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (for example, “Proxy Switchboard Pro”) are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app does receive, from Apple or Google, whether you own the product, so that it can unlock paid features. Their handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

Data sharing

We never sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. The only data that reaches anyone else is what is described above: crash reports to a server we run, ML Kit's technical data to Google, and purchase state through Apple or Google.

Deleting your data

Everything else lives on your device, so uninstalling the app removes it. We do not currently offer a way to request deletion of crash reports already sent; if you would rather send none at all, leave crash reporting off, which is the default.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
