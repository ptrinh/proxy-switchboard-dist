Privacy Policy

Proxy Switchboard · Effective date: 13 June 2026

Proxy Switchboard (“the app”) is a per-destination network router for Android, iOS, and macOS. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is deliberately short because the app collects no personal data.

Summary

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no servers that receive your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Proxy configurations and routing rules (proxy addresses, hostnames, CIDRs, routing modes) are stored only on your device.
  • Secrets — proxy passwords, SSH private keys and passphrases, and WireGuard keys — are stored in the device's secure storage: the Keychain on iOS/macOS, and encrypted storage backed by the Android Keystore on Android. They are shared only between the app and its bundled network extension/VPN service so the proxy engine can use them, and are never sent to us.
  • Configuration is shared between the app and its network extension through a private container on your device.

Network traffic

When you turn the proxy on, the app routes your network traffic according to the rules you configure, sending each connection either directly or through a proxy server that you specify and control. This processing happens entirely on your device. We do not see, inspect, log, store, or transmit your traffic or your browsing history. Any proxy servers you configure are operated by you or by third parties you choose; their handling of traffic is governed by their own policies, not this one.

DNS

To route by hostname, the app answers DNS queries locally on your device and maps names to internal placeholder addresses. These lookups are processed on-device and are not recorded off-device.

Camera (Android)

On Android the app can use the camera, only when you open the QR scanner, to import proxy configurations from QR codes. Frames are processed on-device and are never stored, recorded, or uploaded. The app works fully without camera access.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (for example, “Proxy Switchboard Pro”) are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Their handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies.

Data sharing

None. Because we collect no data, there is nothing to share with third parties, and we never sell data.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy at this URL.
